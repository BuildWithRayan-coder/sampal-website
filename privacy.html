Privacy Policy

We believe clarity should never come at the cost of your privacy. Here is exactly how Sampal handles your data with strict zero-training and zero-storage standards.

Last updated: October 2026

01 Our Core Privacy Philosophy

At Sampal, your trust is our highest priority. We believe software extensions should enhance your daily web browsing without monitoring your life or tracking your footsteps. We strictly adhere to the principle of data minimization: we only process what is strictly required to provide you with on-page clarity and nothing more.

02 Zero Data Storage & Zero AI Training

We Never Train on Your Data: None of the text you highlight, sentences you select, or explanations generated by Sampal are ever saved for training, fine-tuning, or retraining public or private AI models.

When you highlight a piece of text and click "Ask sampal", that excerpt is transmitted via secure, encrypted HTTPS channels to generate your immediate explanation, fact check, or translation. Once the AI response is delivered directly back to your screen, the snippet is discarded from active memory and is never permanently stored on our servers.

03 Browser Permissions & Data Safety

The Sampal browser extension is designed with a lightweight footprint (less than 1 MB) and requests only the minimal permissions required to function:

  • Active Interaction Only: Sampal only activates when you intentionally highlight text on a webpage or trigger the Ask sampal action.
  • No Browsing History Logging: We do not track, record, or store which websites or URLs you visit, how long you stay on pages, or your bookmarks.
  • No Keystroke Tracking: Sampal never monitors background typing, form submissions, or personal inputs.
  • No Advertisers or Data Brokers: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal data to third-party ad networks or brokers.

04 Payment Handling via Freemius

When upgrading to a Sampal premium subscription, all checkout transactions, recurring monthly billing, and invoice distributions are processed securely by Freemius, Inc.

  • PCI-DSS Compliant Security: Freemius handles payments following PCI Level 1 security standards, utilizing tokenized encryption.
  • Zero Card Details Stored by Sampal: We never have access to, receive, or store your credit card numbers, expiration dates, or bank account credentials.
  • Transparent Subscription Management: Freemius provides a dedicated customer portal where you can review billing receipts and easily stop or cancel your automatic monthly subscription anytime.

05 Encryption & Security Practices

All data in transit between your browser and our edge servers is encrypted using modern TLS (Transport Layer Security) 1.2+ protocols with strong cipher suites. Our backend edge functions run in isolated, hardened environments to prevent unauthorized data interception.

06 Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Regardless of where you reside, we recognize and respect your fundamental privacy rights:

  • Right to Know & Access: The right to inquire what minimal data (e.g., license status, support emails) is associated with your account.
  • Right to Deletion: The right to request the complete deletion of your customer record and license history.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We provide full quality of service without selling your personal information.
